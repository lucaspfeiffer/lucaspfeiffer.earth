A set of product design and engineering packages that leverage Open’s ordering primitives to build a completely unique and personalized web ordering experience for any restaurant.

One brand, every surface.

Tap any part of the drawing.

01  Where you start

  • A restaurant, not a look. There is nothing to extend, so brand setup is where the brand gets made — the mark, the type, the colour — before a single surface is built.

  • There is something already: a name, a mark, a colour that half works. This door develops what exists rather than starting the argument over.

  • Fully formed and valued, and ready to be extended. Nothing here gets redesigned. It gets carried into every surface intact, which is the whole reason to make it infrastructure.

02  Brand setup

  • The mark, the typeface, the colour, the spacing and the voice stop being a PDF a designer sends over and become the thing every surface is built from. It is the only place the brand is defined, which is what makes it the only place it has to change.

03  Customer experiences

  • If there is an app or a site the restaurant already loves, the SDK rebuilds it from scratch in a couple of hours. From then on it can be updated as much or as little as the repo it replaced.

  • The site people find before they ever order. Built from the brand rather than from a template with a logo dropped into it.

  • Ordering on the restaurant’s own site, with Open’s ordering primitives underneath and none of the seams a bolted-on ordering page leaves behind.

  • Large orders, lead times and quotes. Same brand, a different job — and usually the surface that gets left on a form nobody has touched in years.

  • The app and the site are built from the same brand, so a customer moving between them never notices the join.

  • The last thing a customer reads, and on most systems the one surface nobody owns. It is a brand surface too.

  • Marketing drawn from the same set, so a campaign looks like the restaurant and not like the tool that sent it.

04  Your tools

  • A small business gets the same tools as the restaurants with the best technology in the world: build custom features, improve conversion, fix what is broken, and maintain the thing without filing a ticket and waiting.

  • Upmarket, the same thing runs the other way: complete customization, developer tools and infrastructure, so a small team ships personalized customer-facing experiences. Better products at less cost.

05  The brand keeps developing

  • Restaurants pay agencies five, six and seven figures to develop a brand over years. The work is rewarding; the waiting is not — rounds of feedback, scheduling, things passed back and forth.

    These are the same tools, self-serve. A team can develop the brand and the way it reaches customers without leaving a loop open for a month, and every surface follows from the one place it is defined.
